01

Location: Mazandaran • Date: summer 2024

North of Iran: A Journey Through Nature and Culture

Ramsar was one of the places I had really wanted to visit in northern Iran, and our trip there in summer 2024 turned out to be such a nice experience. The city has a special atmosphere because you can see the green mountains on one side and the Caspian Sea on the other. Everywhere we went, there was something beautiful to look at. We visited Ramsar Palace first. I really liked the old architecture and the feeling of walking through a place with so much history. After that, we went to the Ramsar cable car, which was probably one of my favorite parts of the trip. Going up while looking at the forest and the sea from above was amazing. The higher we went, the more beautiful the view became, and I took so many photos along the way. Of course, we also spent time by the sea. Walking along the beach, listening to the sound of the waves, and feeling the sea breeze was exactly what I needed after a busy year. The atmosphere was lively, but there were also quiet moments when we could just sit and enjoy the view. What I loved about Ramsar was that it offered a little bit of everything. You could visit historical places, go up into the mountains, walk through green landscapes, and then end the day by the sea. It felt like we had experienced several completely different places during one trip. I really enjoyed my time there, and Ramsar is definitely one of the places in Iran that I would love to return to.

02

Location: Hamedan • Date: summer 2024

Alisadr Cave

A wonderful place I will always remember.

My family and I visited Alisadr Cave in summer 2024, and it became one of the unforgetable trips in my life. We first entered its parking and then we sat in the recreational area. There is a big special bazzar where you can buy everything including local products. If anyone wants to enter the cave, they must buy tickets and wait for their turn. We spent our waiting time with walking, shopping and enjoying the recreational area. There needs some walking until we reach the cave entrace. When we entered the cave, We got amazed at how wonderful that place is. Everything and everywhere was unique and mysterious. We were a group of people exploring the cave, and had a guiding leader. We then got on a boat and continued our journey. The calm water reflected the walls and the soft yellowish lights made the whole place feel almost unreal. We were truly surprised by how gorgeous the cave was with all its beautiful details. The rock formations, calmness, and narrow passages made every turn feel like discovering a completely new place on earth. I remember looking around and thinking about how incredible it was that something so beautiful had been hidden beneath the ground for so long. The boat ride was peaceful and exciting. It was very different from the usual places I visit, and that is exactly what made the experience special. Alisadr Cave was not only truly beautiful to see, but also its is one of the natural wonders of Iran and the world. I REALLY ENJOYED MY TRIP. It is worth visiting multiple times.

03

Namakabrud • Summer 2024

A beautiful town with amazing nature next to the Caspian Sea

Namakabrud was one of those trips where I enjoyed the journey as much as the destination itself. We went there with my family in the summer of 2024, and I still remember how fresh and green everything looked. The combination of the mountains, forest, and the sea made the whole place feel really special. One of my favorite parts was taking the cable car up the mountain. As we slowly went higher, the view kept getting better. Looking down at the huge green forest and seeing the sea in the distance was honestly beautiful. I spent most of the ride just looking outside and taking photos because I didn't want to miss anything. After reaching the top, we walked around, enjoyed the view, and spent some time relaxing. The weather was pleasant, and the fresh mountain air made it even better. It was one of those days when there was nothing I needed to rush to, so I could just enjoy being there with my family. What I liked most about Namakabrud was how much nature there was everywhere. It felt peaceful and refreshing, especially compared to busy cities. I came back from that trip with lots of photos and, more importantly, a really good memory. I would happily go back again, especially just to experience that view one more time.

04

Darband, Tehran • Summer 2023

A vibrant location in the City life of Tehran

I visited Darband with my family during one of our trips to Tehran in 2023, and it became one of my favorite places in the city. As soon as we arrived, the atmosphere felt completely different from the busy streets of Tehran. The sound of the river, the trees, the small shops, and the mountain all came together and made the area feel lively but also peaceful at the same time. We spent a lot of time walking through the narrow streets and exploring the little shops and restaurants along the way. There were so many things to look at, from local food and snacks to small traditional shops. We stopped several times just to sit, talk, and enjoy the surroundings. The further we walked, the more beautiful the mountain views became. What I enjoyed most was how naturally Darband combines nature with the energy of the city. You can spend hours there without feeling like you are in one of the busiest cities in Iran. I especially loved the sound of the water and the cool air coming down from the mountains. It was a simple trip, but it gave me one of those memories that stays with you.

05

Mashahd • July 2022

My trip among the mountains and nature of Mashhad

I still remember how excited I was to get away from the busy streets of Mashhad and spend a day surrounded by nature. We visited Shandiz and Chalidareh during our trip in July 2022, and both places gave us a completely different experience from the city. We started with Shandiz, where the green mountains, trees, and fresh air made the whole area feel peaceful and refreshing. We walked around, enjoyed the scenery, and spent time together without having to rush anywhere. I especially liked the contrast between the warm summer weather and the cooler, greener atmosphere there. Later, we went to Chalidareh, which became one of my favorite parts of the trip. The lake surrounded by mountains was beautiful, and there was so much to see and do around it. We enjoyed the views, spent time near the water, and went on the cable car, which gave us an amazing view of the lake and the surrounding mountains from above. What I loved most about that day was how relaxed everything felt. We were simply walking, talking, taking photos, and enjoying the scenery. Looking back, Shandiz and Chalidareh are some of the places I remember most fondly from that trip.
